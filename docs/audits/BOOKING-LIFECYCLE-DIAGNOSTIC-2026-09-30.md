## Domain truth and authoritative operations

The [BKG3 contract](../../src/lib/bookings/bkg3-atomic-contract.ts) declares one `booking_orders` row per checkout/visit, one or more `booking_attendees`, and one or more `bookings` service-line rows. `bookings.order_id` links a line to its visit; `attendee_id` identifies the recipient; `line_sequence` orders lines. `bookings.customer_id` remains the organizer customer for compatibility; a guest attendee need not have a customer profile. A legacy booking can have `order_id = NULL`. Separate visits by one customer have distinct `booking_orders.id` values. There is no `booking_service_lines` table.

## Operational gaps and safe correction direction

No P0 incident is proven. The following are source-level findings on this working tree; runtime frequency and production exposure are unknown.

1. **P1 — Order-backed cancelled/no-show items remain in Cash Flow payable/count logic.** Reproduction from source: use an order-backed booking with positive remaining balance, then close its line. `getCashFlowData` fetches all today's rows, counts them in payment KPIs, and excludes closed status only for legacy rows in the payable picker ([query](../../src/lib/cash-flow/cash-flow-queries.ts), around lines 290, 401, 781). The repository `post_order_payment_atomic` SQL has no closed-booking guard ([SQL](../../supabase/migrations/20260929140000_cf8_booking_payment_command.sql), lines 90–170). Cancellation updates a single booking status ([operation](../../src/lib/bookings/crm-booking-operations.ts), around lines 516–645) without revising payables. **Verified root cause in source:** asymmetric legacy/order filtering and unchanged payable items. **Policy unknown:** fees or retained deposits after cancellation could be legitimate. Correction should first define per-line/order cancellation financial policy, then make payable presentation and writer honor it; test mixed cancelled/completed siblings, refund/fee cases, and no-show. Do not erase financial history.
2. **P1 — No visit-level Cradle Flow representation for multiple services.** Reproduction from source: one order with three `bookings` lines yields three `CradleFlowTicket` components keyed by booking ID ([workflow](../../src/components/features/crm/today/cradle-flow-workflow.tsx), lines 141–145), and stage/count is calculated per line ([stage](../../src/lib/crm/cradle-flow.ts), lines 76–110). Each ticket receives the same order paid/due state ([today page](../../src/app/(dashboard)/crm/today/page.tsx), lines 121–197), while the ticket displays line service total and order paid/due together ([ticket](../../src/components/features/crm/today/cradle-flow-ticket.tsx), lines 90–100). **Verified root cause:** rendering key/financial scope mismatch. Correction direction: one visit group by `order_id` containing independent service-line states and a single order payment action/summary; retain standalone legacy lines and separate order IDs.
3. **P1 — Cash Flow Day Close presentation is not the existing reconciliation authority.** [Cash Flow query](../../src/lib/cash-flow/cash-flow-queries.ts), around lines 849–878, creates `isBalanced` from zero outstanding bookings and no unmatched payments, sets a fixed `lastUpdatedText`, and marks finalization unsupported. The active [reconciliation workflow](../../src/app/(dashboard)/crm/reconciliation/actions.ts) reads/writes `daily_cash_reconciliations` with expected/actual channel totals and draft/submitted/approved state. **Verified root cause:** two independent read models. Correction direction: inspect consumers and decide authority before any integration. No replacement/migration in this pass.
4. **P1 — Physical cash payment path has no evident open-drawer requirement.** [Order action](../../src/lib/cash-flow/cash-flow-actions.ts), around lines 100–139, delegates directly to payment writer; the [payment SQL](../../supabase/migrations/20260929140000_cf8_booking_payment_command.sql), around lines 188–220 and 408–480, validates cash account type and posts a movement but contains no `cash_sessions` check. Other physical cash adjustment/transfer SQL requires an open session. **Source observation**, deployment unverified. A payment before session opening may not enter the session's time-bounded expected cash ([query](../../src/lib/cash-flow/cash-flow-queries.ts), around lines 229–265). Correction direction: decide whether cash sales require an open drawer and validate the exact drawer/branch transactionally; test close/reopen timing. Do not block digital methods.
5. **P2 — Line counts can be mistaken for visit counts.** [Daily summary](../../src/lib/queries/bookings.ts), lines 596–607, deduplicates amounts by order but increments paid/unpaid counts per service line; the [test](../../tests/lib/bookings/daily-order-payment-summary.test.ts) explicitly expects two unpaid for one two-line order. Cash Flow counts similarly ([query](../../src/lib/cash-flow/cash-flow-queries.ts), lines 401–427). Correction direction: label counts as services or add distinct visit counts, after confirming operational use.
6. **P2 — Customer history and Schedule are line-oriented.** Customer recent activity lists each booking independently ([rail](../../src/components/features/crm/customers/customer-preview-rail.tsx), around lines 360–392); the inspected [schedule query](../../src/lib/queries/schedule.ts), around lines 320–365, does not select `order_id`. Correction direction: preserve independent appointments while displaying an order relationship where it helps; do not merge by customer identity.
7. **P2 — First-payment context for online multi-service orders may collapse service detail.** Before first payment the Cash Flow read model can fall back to one first-service description with the entire order amount if `order_payable_items` do not yet exist ([query](../../src/lib/cash-flow/cash-flow-queries.ts), lines 799–819). **Hypothesis about visible impact:** a cashier may see incomplete item detail until the writer creates payables. Test in a classified environment before changing UI.

## Cash Flow and UI implications

Booking value is captured in line price snapshots and order quote metadata. Expected/payable value is represented by `order_payable_items` and the order financial summary, with quote fallback in read models. Outstanding money is `total payable − net allocated`, floored at zero for UI. Actual collected money appears only after an authorized person records a payment; the order command posts `financial_transactions`, `financial_account_movements`, and `financial_order_allocations`, then derives `partial` or `paid`. Today/ledger totals read posted movements; cash session expected cash uses movements for its drawer after session opening. Existing `daily_cash_reconciliations` stores human-entered actual channel totals and approval state separately from the Cash Flow Day Close display.

The next Booking/Cradle Flow design should show one visit parent for one `order_id`, its multiple independent services/providers/rooms/statuses, and one order financial summary and collection action. It must show two visits when the same customer has two distinct `order_id` values. `customer_id` or customer name is not a grouping key; legacy `order_id = NULL` cannot be grouped speculatively. No UI was changed in this pass.
