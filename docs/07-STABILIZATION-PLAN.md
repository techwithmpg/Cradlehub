# Stabilization Plan

CradleHub is in a controlled stabilization program, not a feature-expansion program. The sequence is governance and live context, current-system truth consolidation, structured diagnostics, scope freeze, UI/UX and workflow planning, then gated corrections.

Issue selection does not occur during C0B. It follows C2 diagnostics and C3 scope freeze, with evidence, severity, owner authorization, and an appropriate test/review gate.

See [development stages](08-DEVELOPMENT-STAGES.md), [stabilization scope](01-STABILIZATION-SCOPE.md), and [known issues](12-KNOWN-ISSUES-REGISTER.md).
