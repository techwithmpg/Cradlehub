# UI/UX Contract

**Status:** NOT YET FROZEN
**Freeze stage:** C4

C0B does not redesign CradleHub or establish a final visual system. Until the C4 contract is frozen, UI work must follow actual workflows and preserve established behavior.

Stable principles:

- reduce staff mistakes and unnecessary clicks;
- make status and next actions clear;
- use consistent interactions and terminology;
- suit the actual desktop/mobile operating context; and
- do not redesign for visual novelty alone.

After C4 approval, stabilization implementation must follow the frozen contract. Changes that affect workflow, permissions, data meaning, or operational safety require the relevant non-UI review as well.
