-- CF8-C — Cash Operations Integrity
--
-- Forward-only correction.
--
-- Protects canonical cash operations at the database authority boundary:
--
-- 1. Physical cash adjustments require an OPEN cash session for the
--    exact drawer and branch. An explicitly open physical drawer remains
--    authoritative across business-date rollover until it is closed.
--
-- 2. Transfers touching a cash drawer require that drawer to have an
--    OPEN cash session.
--
-- 3. Cash operations are PHP-only.
--
-- 4. Idempotent replay validates the original payload shape rather than
--    silently accepting a changed payload under the same key.
--
-- This migration does NOT:
--   - replay historical transactions
--   - modify cash session records
--   - modify existing financial evidence
--   - create financial accounts
