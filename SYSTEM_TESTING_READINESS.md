## 27. Manual test checklist

### A. Normal live clock-in

- Confirm Test Mode is disabled.
- Scan from a connected staff phone.
- Verify `Clocked in`, correct staff, branch, and authoritative time.
- Verify CRM Today and Recent Activity update immediately.
- Verify only one final operational event.

### B. Normal live clock-out

- Scan once.
- Verify `Clocked out` and worked duration.
- Verify CRM updates and no duplicate event.

### C. Test Mode

- Enable Test Mode with a reason.
- Scan from a connected phone.
- Verify the normal scan engine runs and shows `Training Mode · Not live Attendance`.
- Verify `is_test = true`, the Test Mode records filter shows the row, and live totals/payroll do not change.
- Archive test data safely, disable Test Mode, and verify the next scan is live.

### D. Unknown phone

- Clear the test browser’s Attendance connection.
- Scan once and connect with staff credentials.
- Verify the original scan continues to one final CRM event.
- Verify no unnecessary incident is created.

### E. Wrong branch

- Scan another branch QR.
- Verify both branch names and no Attendance change.
- Request correction once and verify the duplicate request is disabled.

### F. Realtime recovery

- Open CRM Today and temporarily disconnect the network.
- Verify Delayed or Offline, restore the network, and verify automatic recovery.
- Verify no duplicate rows, polling intervals, or subscriptions.

### G. Reviewable scan

- Trigger a controlled ambiguous or closing scenario in Test Mode.
- Verify `Scan saved`, `Attendance changed: No`, and one actionable CRM review.
- Verify staff sees no technical details.
